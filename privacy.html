Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026 · DepositCam by Wade Foundry

DepositCam (“the App”) is an Android app that helps renters document the condition of a rental with timestamped, GPS-stamped photos and a PDF report. This policy explains what data is used and where it goes.

Delete your data

DepositCam does not store your walkthroughs or photos on Wade Foundry servers. All of that data stays on your device. To delete it:

  • Uninstall DepositCam from your Android device, or
  • Open Android Settings → Apps → DepositCam → Storage and tap Clear storage / Clear data.

Either action removes walkthroughs, photos, captions, notes, and the local unlock flag for that install. We cannot delete data from your device remotely because we never receive a copy. Google Play purchase records remain with Google under Google’s policies; you can manage Play purchases in your Google account.

Data stored on your device

Walkthroughs, photos, captions, notes, and your one-time PDF unlock status are saved only on your device. We do not host your photos or walkthroughs on Wade Foundry servers.

  • Uninstalling the App removes walkthroughs, photos, and unlock status stored in that install.
  • We cannot restore data that was only stored on your device.

Camera and location

The App may ask for camera access to take photos and for location so GPS coordinates can be burned into photo pixels when available. Location is used at capture time on your device and is not sent to Wade Foundry. You can deny location and still use the App; photos will be timestamped without GPS.

Payments

Optional PDF export unlock is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment method. We do not receive or store your full card number. After a successful purchase (or restore), the App stores an unlock flag on your device so you can export again on that install without paying twice.

Analytics

The App does not use third-party advertising trackers. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

  • App distribution and in-app purchases: Google Play
  • This privacy policy page is hosted as a static page so Google Play can link to it

Google processes payment data under Google’s own privacy policies.

Children

DepositCam is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact Wade Foundry using the support email listed on the DepositCam Google Play store listing.
